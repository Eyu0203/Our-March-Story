Who We Are

Written in the stars

Eyu and Kal are two people whose story began on March 2, 2024, during their school life, at a time when neither of them expected that something simple would grow into something so meaningful. What started as a normal connection slowly turned into a bond that became part of their everyday life, shaping how they see each other, themselves, and the world around them. At first, it was just moments—small conversations, shared looks, and time spent together without realizing how important those moments would become later. But as time passed, those small moments started to build something stronger. They began to enjoy each other’s presence more deeply, finding comfort in talking, laughing, and simply being together even in silence. It was never forced; it naturally grew into something real. As their journey continued, they created many memories that now form the heart of their story. Their first date, first walk together, first kiss, church visits, hotel date, Valentine’s Day celebrations, anniversaries, and countless other moments became milestones they both remember. Even outside of special occasions, they shared everyday experiences that mattered just as much—going out, eating together, taking photos, spending time playing games, and enjoying each other’s company in simple ways that felt special because they were together. They also shared fun and unforgettable activities like playing PS5, going go-karting, playing table tennis, eating pizza, giving flowers, and capturing photos that hold memories of their time together. These moments were not just activities; they were experiences that strengthened their connection and created a sense of togetherness that words alone cannot fully describe. However, their story is not only made of happy moments. Like every real relationship, they also faced misunderstandings, emotional challenges, and times where things felt difficult. Being students and navigating school life together brought its own pressure and distractions, and sometimes outside situations made things more complicated. But instead of allowing those moments to separate them, they used them as lessons. They learned how to listen better, understand each other more deeply, communicate more honestly, and grow with patience. What makes Eyu and Kal’s relationship unique is not perfection, but persistence. It is the choice to stay connected even when things are not easy, to fix instead of walk away, and to keep building something meaningful despite challenges. Their connection is not based only on the good days, but also on how they handle the difficult ones together. Through all of this, they have grown not only as a couple but also as individuals. They have learned about trust, emotional understanding, care, and the importance of choosing each other again and again. Every memory they share—whether big or small—has added another layer to their story. Today, Eyu and Kal’s journey is still ongoing. It is not finished, not perfect, and not predictable—but it is real. It continues to grow with every day they spend together, every conversation they have, and every memory they create. Their story is built on love, effort, shared experiences, and the hope of continuing to write something beautiful together in the future.

She has shared many meaningful memories with him, including anniversaries, dates, celebrations, and everyday moments that have become part of their story. Kal is someone Eyu cares about deeply and continues to choose every day as they grow together.
